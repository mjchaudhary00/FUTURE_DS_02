## Key Insights – Task 2

- Conversion rates across campaign types show limited variation, indicating relatively consistent campaign effectiveness.
- Certain locations generate significantly higher impressions, suggesting stronger brand visibility in those regions.
- Average acquisition cost varies across campaigns, highlighting opportunities for optimizing marketing spend.
- Engagement score provides additional context for evaluating campaigns with similar conversion performance.
- Monthly click trends remain stable, with minor fluctuations reflecting consistent campaign activity over time.

### Business Interpretation
- Campaign performance should be evaluated using multiple metrics rather than conversion rate alone.
- High-impression regions can be prioritized for awareness-driven campaigns.
- Campaigns with high acquisition cost but moderate engagement may require creative or targeting optimization.

### Notes
- The dataset contained ROI as a descriptive field; therefore, performance analysis relied on conversion rate, acquisition cost, and engagement score.
- Duration and target audience fields were treated as contextual attributes rather than numerical measures.
